About Loba

Languages found, never lost.

Loba is a community-built, openly licensed corpus for the endangered and underrepresented languages of East Africa. It starts with Dholuo — spoken by over 4 million people across Kenya and Tanzania — and is designed to grow to Kikuyu, Kamba, Kalenjin, and beyond.

Dholuo remains almost entirely absent from modern NLP tools, translation apps, and AI assistants. Loba exists to change that — by building the foundational dataset needed to create spell checkers, dictionaries, translation engines, and AI tools that actually serve Luo speakers.

How it works

1
Contributors submit entries
Words, phrases, proverbs, and sentences — with translations, example sentences, and cultural context. No linguistics degree required.
2
Community review
Every entry is verified before joining the public corpus. Quality over quantity — a wrong translation poisons downstream AI models.
3
Open forever
All data is released under CC BY 4.0 — free for researchers, educators, and developers to use, forever, as long as they credit the Loba corpus.
